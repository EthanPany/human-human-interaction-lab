# Privacy Policy: Human Human Interaction Lab (HHIL Badge)

Last updated: October 25, 2026

HHIL Badge is a hackathon project from Cal Hacks 13.0 that turns your phone into a live name badge and introduces you to people you both want to meet.

## What we collect
- **Account:** your phone number, name, and an optional password.
- **Profile you choose to share:** role, company, location, tags, hobbies, what you're looking for, what you can help with, how to spot you, avatar choice, and optional photos.
- **Proximity:** while you wear the badge, the app uses Apple's Nearby Interaction (Ultra Wideband) to measure distance and direction to other badge wearers. Only distances between wearers are stored, briefly, to show matches.
- **Conversations (optional):** if recording is on, a conversation with a match is transcribed on your phone and the transcript is sent to our server to write you a short summary and follow up. You can turn recording off in Settings, and the California region asks before every recording.
- **Texts with the agent:** messages you send to the iMessage agent are processed to answer you and update your profile.

## How we use it
To match attendees in both directions, show your badge, send you heads ups, summaries and follow ups, and keep a list of people you met. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Service providers
Data is stored on Convex (or SpacetimeDB, depending on the server the app uses). Profiles, transcripts and texts are processed by Anthropic's Claude API to score matches and write summaries. iMessages are delivered by Photon.
